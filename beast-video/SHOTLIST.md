# SHOT LIST — "This Fucking Beast" (Concept 4: one-night narrative + performance)

One location (dark room / garage / warehouse), one night, phone or mirrorless, one red practical light + ember/orange fill. ~20 shots. Cut to the lyric sync in `index.html`.

## Setup
- **Location:** dark room, black backdrop or bare wall. Chains / rope on wall for the "chains" motif.
- **Light:** one red/orange practical (lamp, LED, firelight effect). Everything else black.
- **Wardrobe:** black. Face half-lit. Optional: beast mask / glowing eyes contact lenses for final chorus.
- **Camera:** locked-off wide + handheld close-ups. Slow push-ins. No fancy moves needed.
